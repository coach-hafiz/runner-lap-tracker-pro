How To Use Pro

Recommended workflow for PE teachers.

Pro Tracker NAPFA Feedback How To Use
  1. Upload your class list. Recommended columns: Index, Name, Gender, Age, Class, Teacher, Current Time.
  2. Check that Class and Staff / Teacher have been auto-filled correctly.
  3. Select the students running. Selected cards turn green.
  4. Select number of laps and distance, then tap Confirm Runners.
  5. Tap Start to begin the timer.
  6. Tap the runner panel whenever the runner completes one lap.
  7. Do nothing for runners who do not finish. They will be marked DNF after Stop.
  8. Tap Undo if you accidentally tap the wrong runner or double-tap.
  9. Tap Stop to display summary and download CSV.
  10. Open NAPFA Feedback to view each student’s grade and time needed for the next grade.
